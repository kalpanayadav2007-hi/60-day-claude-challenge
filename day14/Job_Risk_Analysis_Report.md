## 5 Smart Interview Questions

1. **"Since the title is Junior Developer but the listing mentions 5+ years of experience — what does the actual day-one expectation look like for someone earlier in their career?"**
 *Tests whether the JD is inflated/inaccurate and how self-aware the hiring team is about it.*

2. **"Can you walk me through what a typical week looks like, including any on-call or after-hours expectations?"**
 *Directly probes the 24/7 support claim and surfaces real working hours.*

3. **"The listing says remote with possible relocation to Bengaluru after probation — what determines whether relocation becomes mandatory, and for whom?"**
 *Forces clarity on the hidden-office/relocation risk before signing anything.*

4. **"What's the compensation range for this role, and how is it benchmarked?"**
 *Tests salary transparency — hesitation or deflection is itself informative.*

5. **"How long has the current engineering team been in place, and has anyone left the team in the last few months?"**
 *Indirect churn/retention check, given the reviews mentioning long hours.*
