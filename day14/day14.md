# Day 14 – Job Red Flag Detector

## Objective
Learned how to analyze a job opportunity using Claude's Job Red Flag Detector.

## Steps Completed
- Read the provided resources
- Watched the solution video
- Opened Claude
- Set effort level to Low
- Collected a Job Description
- Gathered company information
- Pasted the Job Red Flag Detector prompt
- Generated the risk analysis report
- Reviewed:
  - Overall Risk Score
  - Red Flags
  - Positive Signals
  - Risk Breakdown
  - Final Verdict
  - Interview Questions
- Captured screenshots

## Key Learnings
- Learned how to identify warning signs in job descriptions.
- Understood how company information can affect job risk.
- Learned to evaluate employers before applying.
- Discovered useful interview questions to ask recruiters.

## Files Included

- Generated Report

Successfully completed the Day 14 challenge and documented the results.
