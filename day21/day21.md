## Privacy Findings

- The dashboard visualizes the amount of personal information exposed online.
- It identifies which companies potentially hold user data.
- The Privacy Score provides an estimate of overall digital privacy.
- The Exposure Heatmap highlights areas with higher privacy risks.
- The Risk Radar identifies categories requiring attention.
- The Privacy Improvement Simulator demonstrates how specific actions can improve the overall privacy score.
