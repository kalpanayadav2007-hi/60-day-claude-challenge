# Day 22 – Startup Validation with Claude AI

## Objective
Validate a startup idea using Claude AI by analyzing founder-market fit, market size, competitors, risks, and creating a 30-day action plan.

## Steps Completed

- Read the provided resources.
- Watched the solution video.
- Opened Claude AI.
- Set the effort level to Medium/High.
- Started a new conversation.
- Pasted the Startup Validation prompt.
- Answered all startup discovery questions honestly.
- Provided details about the startup idea and target customers.
- Shared existing validation (users, interviews, feedback, or waitlist if available).
- Reviewed the Founder-Market Fit analysis.
- Reviewed the TAM, SAM, and SOM calculations.
- Analyzed the Competitor Analysis.
- Studied the Market Gap Analysis.
- Reviewed the Customer Persona and Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).
- Analyzed the Risk Assessment.
- Reviewed the Go / No-Go recommendation.
- Studied the 30-Day Action Plan.
- Exported and saved the Startup Validation Report.
- Captured screenshots of the important insights.

---

## Key Learnings

- Learned how to validate a startup idea before investing significant time or money.
- Understood the importance of Founder-Market Fit.
- Learned how TAM, SAM, and SOM estimate market opportunities.
- Identified competitors and market gaps.
- Understood how customer personas help target the right audience.
- Learned to assess business risks and prioritize them.
- Received a structured 30-day action plan for validating the startup idea.
