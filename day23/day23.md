# Day 23 – Customer & MVP Blueprint

## Objective
Generate a Customer & MVP Blueprint using Claude AI based on the startup idea or the Startup Validation Report from Day 22.

---

## Steps Completed

- Read the provided resources.
- Watched the solution video.
- Opened Claude AI.
- Set Claude effort level to **Low**.
- Continued the conversation from yesterday.
- Uploaded the Startup Validation Report from Day 22 (or provided startup details).
- Pasted the Customer & MVP Blueprint prompt.
- Generated the complete Customer & MVP Blueprint report.
- Reviewed the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).
- Analyzed the Buyer Persona.
- Reviewed the Top 10 Customer Pain Points.
- Studied the Customer Journey Map.
- Reviewed Buying Triggers and Customer Objections.
- Analyzed the MVP Recommendation.
- Reviewed MoSCoW Prioritization.
- Studied the Pricing Hypothesis.
- Analyzed the Top 5 Risks.
- Reviewed the Founder Action Sheet.
- Saved/exported the generated report.
- Captured screenshots of important sections.
