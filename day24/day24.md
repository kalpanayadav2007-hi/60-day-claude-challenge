# Day 24 – Business Strategy Report

## Objective
Generate a complete Business Strategy Report from the Day 23 Customer & MVP Blueprint.

## Sections Reviewed
- Business Reality Check
- Business Model Canvas
- Revenue & Pricing Strategy
- Go-To-Market Strategy
- Customer Acquisition Strategy
- First 100 Users Plan
- Competitive Position & Moat
- Reverse SWOT Analysis
- Investor One-Liner
- Founder Pitch
- Investment Scorecard
- Visual Dashboard
- Final Verdict

## Key Learnings
- A business model should be validated before scaling.
- Customer acquisition channels should be prioritized based on ROI.
- Revenue strategy must align with customer willingness to pay.
- Competitive moats should be sustainable and difficult to replicate.
- SWOT analysis helps identify hidden risks and opportunities.
