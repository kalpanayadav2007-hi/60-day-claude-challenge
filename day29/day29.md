# Day 29 — Operation Lifeline

## 🚨 Overview

Operation Lifeline is an AI-powered supply chain crisis simulation created using Claude, React, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

The simulation generates a company and crisis scenario where the user must make strategic business decisions.

## 🎯 Activities

* Generated and reviewed a random company profile.
* Analyzed the assigned supply chain crisis.
* Selected 3 crisis response actions.
* Completed supplier negotiation rounds.
* Answered CEO leadership questions.
* Selected 2 AI supply chain investments.
* Reviewed the final executive dashboard and scores.
* Replayed the simulation with different scenarios.

## 🧠 Key Learnings

* Effective crisis management requires quick and strategic decisions.
* Supplier reliability is as important as cost.
* AI can improve forecasting, risk management, and supply chain resilience.
* Leadership and communication are critical during disruptions.
* Different decisions can produce significantly different business outcomes.
