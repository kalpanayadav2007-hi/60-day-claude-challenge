# Day 30 — Supply Chain Optimization with Claude

## 🚀 Challenge

Build a **Supply Chain Builder** that helps beginners understand how supply-chain decisions affect a real business.

The application simulates a company and allows users to build and optimize its supply chain by making strategic decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The main objective of this challenge was to understand how supply-chain decisions involve trade-offs between:

* Cost
* Delivery Speed
* Risk
* Customer Satisfaction
* Sustainability

The goal was to create an interactive simulator where every decision changes the business metrics.

---

## 🛠️ What I Built

I built a single-file React application called **Supply Chain Builder**.

The application:

1. Introduces the basic concept of supply chains.
2. Generates a random company.
3. Shows the company's industry, products, countries served, and demand level.
4. Guides the user through multiple supply-chain decisions.
5. Updates business metrics after every decision.
6. Generates a final optimization dashboard.

---

## 🏭 Supply Chain Decisions

### 1. Supplier Strategy

The user chooses between:

* Single Supplier
* Multiple Suppliers

This demonstrates the trade-off between lower complexity/cost and better resilience.

### 2. Factory Location

The user selects a factory location and observes how the decision affects cost, delivery speed, and risk.

### 3. Warehouse Strategy

The user chooses an appropriate warehouse strategy based on the company's requirements.

### 4. Transportation

Available transportation methods include:

* Road
* Rail
* Sea
* Air

Each option has different cost, speed, risk, and sustainability characteristics.

### 5. Inventory Strategy

The user chooses between:

* Low Inventory
* Balanced Inventory
* High Inventory

This demonstrates the relationship between inventory cost and product availability.

---

## 📊 Live Business Metrics

The application dynamically updates:

* 💰 Cost
* 🚚 Delivery Speed
* ⚠️ Risk
* 😊 Customer Satisfaction
* 🌱 Sustainability

This makes the impact of each decision immediately visible.

---

## 🏆 Final Dashboard

At the end of each simulation, the application generates:

* Overall Supply Chain Score
* Strengths
* Weaknesses
* Biggest Risk
* Three Practical Improvements

The user can also replay the simulation with a new randomly generated company.

## 🧠 Key Learnings

### 1. Supply-chain optimization is about trade-offs

Optimizing one metric does not always produce the best overall result. A cheaper option can increase risk or reduce delivery speed.

### 2. Supplier diversification improves resilience

Multiple suppliers can reduce dependency on a single source and help protect the business from disruptions.

### 3. Transportation affects business performance

Fast transportation can improve customer satisfaction but usually increases cost, while slower transportation can reduce cost but affect delivery speed.

### 4. Inventory needs to be balanced

Keeping too little inventory can cause stockouts, while keeping too much increases storage and holding costs.

### 5. Business decisions are interconnected

A single supply-chain decision can affect several business metrics at the same time.
