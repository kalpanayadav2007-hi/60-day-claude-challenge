# Day 31 – AI Supply Chain Control Tower

## 🚀 60 Days Claude AI Challenge

### 📌 Project Overview

For Day 31 of the 60 Days Claude AI Challenge, I built and explored an **AI Supply Chain Control Tower** using Claude.

The application simulates real-world supply chain operations where users monitor incoming operational alerts, prioritize incidents, select corrective actions, and observe how their decisions affect key performance indicators (KPIs).

The goal of this challenge was to understand how AI can support **real-time operational decision-making, incident prioritization, and supply chain management**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Monitor multiple supply chain alerts simultaneously.
* Identify and prioritize critical incidents.
* Evaluate different corrective actions.
* Understand the impact of operational decisions.
* Track KPI changes throughout the simulation.
* Improve decision-making through simulation replay.
* Analyze final operational performance.

## 🧠 Key Learnings

### 1. AI-Assisted Application Development

Claude can generate a complete functional HTML application from a detailed natural-language prompt, significantly reducing development time.

### 2. Operational Prioritization

Not every incident has the same business impact. Effective supply chain management requires prioritizing issues based on factors such as:

* Urgency
* Business impact
* Customer impact
* Financial impact
* Operational risk

### 3. Data-Driven Decision Making

The simulation demonstrated how operational decisions can directly affect KPIs. This highlights the importance of evaluating the potential consequences of an action before implementing it.

### 4. Managing Multiple Alerts

A real supply chain environment can generate multiple simultaneous alerts. A control tower helps operators maintain visibility and decide which issues require immediate attention.

### 5. Simulation-Based Learning

Replaying the simulation provides an opportunity to analyze previous decisions, identify mistakes, and improve the final operational score.

## 💡 Key Takeaway

The AI Supply Chain Control Tower demonstrates how AI-assisted applications can help organizations make faster and more informed operational decisions.
