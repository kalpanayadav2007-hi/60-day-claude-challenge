## Key Learnings

* Learned how to identify the correct owner for a workplace task instead of automatically taking responsibility for everything.
* Improved understanding of **task routing** and how work should move between different roles or departments.
* Learned that effective collaboration requires clear ownership and communication.
* Understood the importance of considering organizational structure before making decisions.
* Practiced breaking down workplace problems into smaller decisions.
* Learned how interactive applications can make organizational and problem-solving concepts more engaging.
* Experienced how AI can be used to quickly generate a functional learning application from a structured prompt.
