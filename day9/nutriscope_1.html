      Nutrition sources & methodology

      Where the numbers in NutriScope come from, and how targets are calculated.

      Reference data

      Food composition
      Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT 2017)
      Primary reference for Indian staples, dals, and regional dishes — National Institute of Nutrition, ICMR.
      USDA FoodData Central
      Cross-reference for fruits, nuts, seeds, and packaged staples.
      ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians (2020)
      Basis for macro split ratios and general portion sizing.

      All values in this build are rounded, typical-serving estimates for common preparations — actual nutrient content varies with brand, cooking method, and portion.

      Target formulas

      Methodology
      Energy — Mifflin-St Jeor equation
      BMR × activity multiplier (1.2–1.9 depending on activity level).
      Protein
      1.6 g per kg body weight — within the general active-adult range.
      Fat / Carbs
      Fat set to 25% of total energy; carbohydrates fill the remainder.
      Micronutrients
      RDA reference values for iron, calcium, vitamin C/D/B12/A/B6, folate, magnesium, zinc and potassium, gender-adjusted where applicable.

      Full disclaimer

      Please read

      NutriScope is an educational self-tracking tool. It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, or replace advice from a registered dietitian, nutritionist, or physician. Nutrient values are typical estimates for common food preparations and can vary meaningfully based on ingredient source, cooking method, and portion size. Risk flags in this app describe statistical associations between low or high nutrient intake and common conditions — they are not a medical assessment of you personally. If you have a diagnosed health condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or are making significant dietary changes, please consult a qualified professional first.
